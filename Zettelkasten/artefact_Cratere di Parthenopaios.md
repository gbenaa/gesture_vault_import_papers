# Description
Apulian red-figure calyx-krater dating to the 4th century BC, showing a complex mythological scene often interpreted as a theatrical representation.  
- **Upper register:** Hermes, Apollo, and Ares appear.  
- **Main scene:** Parthenopaios is shown reclining on a kline, visited by an elder (possibly Tiresias). Behind him stands his mother, Atalanta.  
The krater illustrates mythological themes linked with tragedy and the Theban cycle, typical of South Italian vase painting of the late Classical period.

Parthenopaios - Dionysos with satyr and maenads (see below)
