# Fayum Portrait of a Man with a Cup

# Period
ca. 225–250 AD  

# Caption
Funerary Portrait of a Man with a Cup  

# Description
A funerary portrait of a man holding a cup, executed in wax paint on wood. Likely from Antinopolis, Egypt. The work exemplifies the Fayum portrait tradition, blending Greco-Roman artistic techniques with Egyptian funerary customs.
