# Period
Roman Period, ca. 230–250 AD

# Caption
Human mummy, burial-cloth, and coffin of a young boy

# Description
Mummified remains of a boy, enclosed in a painted shroud and a vaulted wooden coffin.  
- **Sex:** male  
- **Age:** 2–5 years  
- **Stature:** unknown  

**Head and torso** – Head tilted forward slightly. The head and torso appear to be fully articulated, with some soft tissues remaining.  
**Arms** – Extended. Hands, with fingers extended, palms in contact with the outer surface of the body.  

**Materials:** human tissue, linen, wood  
**Technique:** tempera, painted

# Quotes
Salima Ikram describes a 20th-century Egyptian custom of shaving young boys who were unwell, except for a few tufts of hair. If the child was cured, the tufts would be shaved off in celebration. Ikram refers to hair enclosed in clay balls (as found in Amarna) and wonders whether the hairstyle reflects ancient Egyptian beliefs.
